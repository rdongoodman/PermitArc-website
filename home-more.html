Common questions

What does document scanning auto-fill?

PermitArc extracts critical fields including document type, issuing agency, effective date, and expiration date. You can review, edit, and add custom notes before saving.

What devices does PermitArc support?

PermitArc runs on Android, Windows, iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Snap photos directly with your camera, or upload PDFs and images seamlessly from your phone, tablet, or desktop.

What is included with my monthly subscription?

Every plan includes automated Expiration Radar alerts, Weekly Patrol regulatory scans for your ZIP code, Proactive Gap Reviews, and access to on-demand AI document Q&A.

Is PermitArc only for restaurants?

No. While built to handle the intense compliance requirements of commercial kitchens, PermitArc supports any brick-and-mortar storefront—including auto repair, retail, salons, fitness centers, and child care facilities.

Does PermitArc file renewals or provide legal advice?

No. PermitArc is an automated monitoring and tracking platform, not a legal or filing service. We track your deadlines, monitor code updates, and provide direct links to renewal portals so you maintain full control.
