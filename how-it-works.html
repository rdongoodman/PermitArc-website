What to expect when you scan

PermitArc stores your full document. The scan review form auto-fills the fields we need for expiration alerts, weekly patrol, proactive vault checks, and AI answers about your shop — not every line on your document. Add anything else with + Add field or + Add note.

Upload more → smarter AI → fewer repeat nags

The more licenses, insurance certificates, and permits you upload for each shop, the better Proactive Compliance understands what's already on file. Few documents on file means more “you may be missing…” suggestions. A fuller compliance binder gets quieter, sharper reviews — and the same suggestion won't spam you week after week once you've addressed it or uploaded the document.

Reading alerts (important)

Hide on map — then read

Tapping X on a proactive map strip hides it until you open the full brief in Intel Deck. Your unread badge stays until you read there.

View full brief

In Patrol or Proactive lists, tap View full brief to read and mark an item read. Scrolling past a row does not count as reading.

Mark all read

The Mark all read (checkmark) icon in an inbox clears everything at once if you're caught up.

Quick FAQ

Why did I get email but no push?

Push must be ON in Communication Profile and your phone must allow notifications for PermitArc. Email and in-app still work if push is off.

Why no proactive suggestion this week?

Quiet weeks are normal when your uploaded documents haven't changed or nothing new applies to your business type and area. Patrol can also be quiet when no official changes appear.

Will it keep telling me about the same grease trap / food handler cert?

Once you upload the document or we've already suggested the same item, Proactive suppresses repeats. Upload more documents for that shop and suggestions drop sharply.

Is this legal advice?

No. PermitArc summarizes your documents and public sources. Verify with officials before you rely on any date or requirement.
