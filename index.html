Common questions

What does scan auto-fill?

PermitArc stores your full document. The scan review form auto-fills the fields we need for Expiration Radar, Weekly Patrol, Proactive Review, and AI answers about your shop — not every line on your document. Add anything else with + Add field or + Add note.

Quick guide — scan and Intel Deck →

What devices does PermitArc run on?

Android phone & tablet and Windows PC & laptop at launch. iPhone, iPad & Mac — v1.1. Same account on each device you use. Scan with camera, gallery, or upload a PDF where supported.

What's included every month at no extra charge?

Expiration Radar (30 / 15 / 5 day warnings), Weekly Patrol, and Proactive Review per storefront. Chat, voice, and scanning use a separate allowance that scales with each location. Full breakdown on pricing →

Is PermitArc only for restaurants?

We lead with restaurant pain — but any storefront with permits and licenses can use PermitArc. Pick your business type when you add a location.

Does PermitArc file renewals for me?

No. We watch, remind, and help you understand what's due. You stay in control.
